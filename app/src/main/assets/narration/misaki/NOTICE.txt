The Android adaptation ports Misaki's dictionary lookup, morphology, stress and
right-to-left phonetic context to Kotlin. Dictionaries are optional installed
language resources, not included in the base APK. Their exact sizes and SHA-256
digests are recorded in EnglishMisakiResources.

The native adaptation uses the original spaCy part-of-speech frontend and
PeterReid US/GB BART fallback models, converted locally to full float32 ONNX.
These resources are optional installations, not bundled into the base APK.
Their separate MIT and Apache 2.0 notices are available in the reading licenses
screen; static SHA-256 digests are recorded in EnglishAuxResources.

No eSpeak NG, phonemizer-fork or num2words is distributed by this adaptation.
Number expansion is independently implemented. Missing resources or unsupported
input produce a controlled error and use the existing Android TextToSpeech
alternative. The production runtime does not require Python or spaCy binaries.
